# HostilePet — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

**Version:** 1.0
**Duration:** 2:00
**Format:** 16:9, 1080p or 1440p
**Editing tool:** CapCut Desktop
**Purpose:** Establish the cost of distraction first, then introduce HostilePet as an opt-in, playful focus companion and demonstrate the MVP flow.

## Creative Direction

The first minute is human and cinematic: calm focus becomes an increasingly crowded, dopamine-driven distraction loop. The pet does not appear until that problem is clear. The second minute is a real product demonstration. The tone is playful and blunt, never abusive or coercive.

The product footage must show only supported MVP behavior: a Focus session, allowed work apps, escalating pet states, reversible interventions, and optional/local settings. Do not claim that the app blocks credit cards, closes tabs, monitors users secretly, or permanently locks their device.

## Edit and Asset Notes / Ghi chú dựng và asset

- **Opening (0:00–0:48):** Film the person and the phone interaction separately from the screen recording. Add mock chat, video, and shopping cards as overlays; do not use private real messages or account data.
- **Timer:** Keep it visible in the same place throughout the opening. Use white or cool green while focused; change to red at `0:22`, then accelerate it to `01:00`.
- **Distraction cards:** Add one card at a time. Use position and scale keyframes so they enter from the left and right, then crowd the frame without obscuring the subject entirely.
- **Audio:** Start with keyboard ambience. Layer notification, scroll, and swipe sounds as cards accumulate. Make a clean music transition at `0:48` when the pet appears. Use captions for every spoken line.
- **Product section (1:00–2:00):** Use actual app screen recordings wherever possible. If a behavior is not implemented yet, label it as a concept instead of presenting it as live functionality.
- **Pet copy:** Keep it short, funny, and non-insulting. Suggested Concerned line: “That tab looks suspiciously unrelated to your goal.” Suggested Warning line: “Pick one: back to work, a real break, or end the session.”

## Production Checklist / Danh sách chuẩn bị

1. Film the focused worker, phone pickup, scrolling, and return-to-work shots.
2. Prepare three fictional distraction overlays: a chat, a short-form video, and a shopping page.
3. Capture the live HostilePet Focus flow from menu bar through Calm, Concerned, Warning, Hostile, and Return to Focus.
4. Export or create four pet states: Calm, Concerned, Warning, and Hostile.
5. Record the Vietnamese and English voice-overs separately; make one language the main narration and use the other as subtitles or an alternate export.
6. Build the edit in CapCut, then review it once with sound off (visual clarity) and once without looking at the screen (voice and sound clarity).
